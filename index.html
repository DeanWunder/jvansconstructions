Our Services

A full range of residential and commercial carpentry and construction services, backed by honest advice and tidy workmanship.

Cladding / Eaves

Supply and installation of new cladding, Repair / remediate existing cladding, Build / remediate existing worn eaves.

Granny Flat Renovations

Full renovations and structural upgrades for granny flats, from framing through to finishing touches.

Window / Doors

Supply and installation of new doors and windows, Supply and installation of new locks and hardware, Repair / Remediate worn, wet rotten, leaking windows or doors.

Wall & Roof Framing

Remedial works to existing roof structure, Create new openings for new windows or doors, Create new open living areas taking out structural walls.

Subfloor Framing

Remedial works to damaged bearers and joists, Rebuilding new piers to support your home.

Decks & Pergolas

Hardwood and composite decking, Custom pergolas and carports.

Property Maintenance / Remedial Works

Wall and Roof repairs, water damage repairs, wet calls 24/7 on leaking roofs, Replacing barge / fascia boards.

Roofing

Supply and installation of new metal roof, Replacing wet rotten, damaged fascia & barge boards to give that fresh look, Supply and installation of skylights.

Termite / Timber Repairs

Replacement of termite damaged ceiling joists, floor joists and walls, Restoring safety and integrity of your home.

General Carpentry

Skirting, architraves, bulkheads, cabinetry, fitouts of linen cupboards and wardrobes.
